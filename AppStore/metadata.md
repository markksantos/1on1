## Description

A private one-on-one messenger that actually demands your attention.

Tired of important messages getting buried under work Slacks, group chats, and notification spam? 1on1 is built for two people — a partner, a teammate, a parent — who want to actually hear from each other.

When a message arrives, it appears as a large, unmissable floating overlay in the center of your screen. No banner that disappears in two seconds. No buried notification badge. Just a panel that gently pulses until you read it or reply.

Designed for life. When you're nearby, 1on1 connects directly over your local network — fast, private, no servers in the middle. When you're on different networks, it relays through end-to-end encrypted CloudKit so your conversation never stops.

FEATURES
• Floating message overlays that demand attention
• Lives in your menu bar — always one click away
• Local peer-to-peer messaging on the same Wi-Fi
• End-to-end encrypted relay over the internet
• Screenshot sharing with a single keystroke (Cmd-Shift-2)
• Quick compose hotkey (Cmd-Shift-1)
• Quiet hours so you don't wake them up
• Typing indicators and read receipts
• Searchable message history
• Each user has a unique code — share it once, never type a name again

PRIVACY
1on1 has no servers, no analytics, and no accounts. Your messages travel only between you and your partner — directly when you're nearby, encrypted through your own iCloud when you're not. We can't read your messages because we never see them.

DESIGNED FOR macOS
Native SwiftUI throughout. Menu bar app, no Dock clutter. Hardened runtime, sandboxed, signed.

## App Review Information notes (private, for the reviewer)
1on1 is a peer-to-peer messenger designed for two devices to message each other.

To test the app fully:
1. Launch 1on1. The Welcome screen will guide you through picking a display name and copying your unique code.
2. To exercise messaging, you'd normally enter your partner's code on the Connection tab in Settings. To test alone, you can install the app on a second Mac signed into the same iCloud account, copy your code from one Mac, paste it into the Connection tab on the other, and send a test message — both devices will receive each other's messages.
3. Cmd-Shift-1 opens a quick compose popover.
4. Cmd-Shift-2 captures and shares a screenshot (requires Screen Recording permission).

The CloudKit relay uses the public CloudKit database for the app's container with end-to-end encryption (HKDF-derived keys from the shared room code, AES-GCM payloads). No personally identifiable data is stored unencrypted.
